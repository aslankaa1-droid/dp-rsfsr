Financial Model — Assumptions

Автор: Кагиров А.-Х.А. · Свидетельство о депонировании №4011265 от 19.12.2024 · Center Group Company · Engagement: CPFSR-RF-DESIGN-2026

Financial model of CP RSFSR — basis of the assumptions

Note. This is a translation of the Russian original. In case of any discrepancy in wording, the Russian version prevails. All figures are taken from the same calculation engine.

Document: explanatory note accompanying the financial model. Related files: Финансовая_модель_ЦП_РСФСР.md (the calculation itself), Модель/assumptions.json (all input values), Модель/Выгрузка/*.csv (machine-readable export). Author of the concept: Kagirov A.-Kh.A., deposit certificate No. 4011265 of 19.12.2024 — fixing the priority date and the hash; protection regime — know-how (Art. 1465 Civil Code) plus computer programme (Art. 1262 Civil Code). Edition: v5 of 07.09.2026.

The purpose of this note is to show where every value comes from, and to name honestly what rests on nothing but the author's design.

The calculation is separated from the text. The figures live in Модель/assumptions.json and are computed by the engine Модель/model.py. Documents are assembled from the engine. This was done deliberately: in earlier editions the figures were stored separately in each document and drifted apart.

1. The market

1.1. Collateral capacity of RUB 25 tn

The logic of the constraint: only 10% of assets are drawn into the system, and for collateral purposes value is taken at 50% of market value — which corresponds to the practice of collateral haircuts.

What this is not. It is an estimate of potential capacity, not of demand. The project contains no evidence that owners are willing to pledge property on these terms. Verifying demand is the first thing that should be done before any conversation about money.

1.2. Capacity indexation of 4% a year

The Bank of Russia inflation target. There is no real market growth in the model — only revaluation.

1.3. Trajectory of the platform's share

The author lowered the year-ten target from 20% to 18% following the 2026 audit. The conservative scenario is 7%, the optimistic one 25%.

This is the model's principal unverified assumption. It rests on no pilot, no contracts and no comparable project. All the remaining arithmetic is derived from this line. In a discussion with an investor the conversation should start here, not with the resulting value.

1.4. The horizon starts in 2027

The previous edition counted from 2025 and assumed CFA issuance of RUB 104 bn as early as 2026. At the date of this edition there is no information-system operator licence, no adopted regulatory framework and no issue at all. The horizon has therefore been shifted: 2027 for preparation, 2028 for the first issues.

The price of that shift is visible in the calculation: every year of delay removes roughly a quarter of enterprise value.

1.5. The circulating stack

Stack of year t = the sum of issues over years t−4 … t. The average life of an issue is 5 years, which corresponds to the length of infrastructure cycles.

2.1. Why appraisal and insurance are recognised net

In this line the platform acts as an agent: it collects 0.8% of volume and passes 85% of that on to appraisers and insurers. Its own margin is 0.12% of volume. Only that margin enters revenue; gross billings are shown on a separate memorandum line.

In the previous edition the whole 0.8% was counted as platform revenue. In 2034 that produced RUB 51,240 mn — more than a third of all stated revenue — while the offsetting costs never reached the cost side. The note "pass-through, 85% offsetting costs" was present in the memorandum to the model but was never carried into the calculation.

2.2. Tariffs are not indexed

A deliberate simplification in favour of conservatism: revenue growth comes solely from volume growth.

4. Operating costs

Costs are assembled from two parts.

Payroll = year-end headcount × average salary × 12 × 1.30 (social contributions of 30%). Headcount 40 → 370, first-year salary RUB 350 thousand per month, indexed at 6% a year.

Other costs = a base of RUB 502 mn indexed at 4% a year + a variable part of 22% of revenue.

The first-year base breaks down as follows:

ItemRUB mn

The variable 22% of revenue covers acquiring, infrastructure under load, compliance, legal support of volume and customer acquisition. The level is calibrated so that the mature-year margin sits within the range of comparable infrastructure operators (65–75%). The result is 75.1% in 2036.

In the previous edition payroll was not derived from headcount: the 2029 line corresponded to roughly twice the stated headcount, and the 2026 line to roughly half of it. At the same time the total costs of the second year (RUB 820 mn) did not even cover the payroll of the stated 120 people (RUB 695 mn), leaving RUB 125 mn for everything else against RUB 502 mn a year earlier.

What the costs do not contain. No separate line for customer acquisition; no capital requirements for an information-system operator; no reserves. That is a limitation of the model, not a conclusion that such costs will not arise.

Earlier editions used a rate of 20% — it ceased to apply from 2025.

The profit-tax relief for accredited IT organisations and the reduced social contributions are not built into the base case: that is upside, not an assumption.

VAT. Transactions in digital financial assets are not subject to value added tax: such assets are classified as securities, and the sale of securities is VAT-exempt (sub-clause 12, clause 2, Art. 149 of the Tax Code). The position was confirmed by the owner of the project on 07.09.2026.

6. The discount rate

The method of construction is a build-up of risk premiums on top of the rouble risk-free rate.

ComponentValueSource
Risk-free rate (10-year OFZ)14.73%market data as at 21.05.2026
Equity risk premium × beta (8.13% × 1.15)9.35%Damodaran
Stage premium2.50%IPEV Guidelines
Illiquidity premium3.00%IPEV Guidelines
Total29.58%

A country risk premium is not added separately: it is already contained in the rouble risk-free rate. Adding it as a second layer would be double counting.

The defensible interval is 27–33%; it is used as the sensitivity range.

In the previous edition the same calculation appeared in two documents with different components (2.5% + 3.0% against 1.5% + 2.5%) and with a different equity risk premium (8.13% against 9%), while one passage stated outright that "the base case uses 30%". The resulting figure was nevertheless quoted everywhere as 29.5%.

7. Terminal value

A single method — the Gordon model on a normalised cash flow:

  • in the steady state capital expenditure is set equal to depreciation;
  • the increase in working capital equals the growth rate multiplied by working capital;
  • the growth rate g = 4% is nominal, equal to the inflation target, that is zero real growth.

The share of terminal value in the valuation is 38%. That is within the normal range for a growing infrastructure company but at its upper edge; in a discussion with an investor this share should be stated openly.

Earlier editions used three incompatible approaches at once: a single-stage Gordon model with g=2% in the financial model, a three-stage model in the calculation table, and g=4% in the business plan. The review of 10.06.2026 acknowledged that terminal value had been understated roughly threefold, but the correction was made only in the text, not in the calculation.

8. Working capital

5% of revenue — a usual assumption for financial operators (payment systems in Russia 3–7%, depositories 5–10%).

9. Sensitivity and simulation

Computed programmatically:

  • discount rate × market share (a 5 × 5 grid);
  • deviation of operating costs from −10% to +30%;
  • launch delay of 0/1/2/3 years;
  • simulation, 2,000 iterations, generator with a fixed seed — the result is reproducible.

The principal conclusion of the sensitivity analysis: costs barely affect value (a 30% increase changes it by 8%), while timing is decisive (a year of delay costs a quarter of the value).

Earlier editions quoted results of a simulation — a median of RUB 94 bn, a P10–P90 interval of RUB 42–168 bn, a probability of negative value of 4.2% — while no such calculation existed; in two documents the same figures differed (a median of 91 against 94). The simulation is now genuinely performed, and its median is RUB 39.2 bn: it allows for the probability of a launch delay, which earlier estimates did not.

10. What the model does not include, and why

  1. State subsidies — upside, not an assumption.
  2. Debt financing — the model is equity-only; a mixed structure would add an interest tax shield.
  3. Currency risk — all flows are in roubles.
  4. Competition for collateral — the base case assumes the category is unique in the early years; the risk is covered by the conservative scenario.
  5. A refusal by the regulator — disclosed in 04_Бизнес-план/Wedge_pilot-light.md; in that case the conservative scenario applies (enterprise value RUB 20.1 bn).
  6. Capital requirements for an information-system operator — not modelled; they require verification against Federal Law 259-FZ and the regulations of the Bank of Russia.
  7. Customer acquisition cost — not shown as a separate line.
