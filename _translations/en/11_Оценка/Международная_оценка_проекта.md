**Approach:** triangulation of four independent methods with weighted reconciliation and a single (one-stage) discount.
**Source of market parameters:** `_Мастер/Единые_показатели_v2.md` (CBR rate 14.5%, FX 71.55 RUB/USD, ERP RF 8.13%) — single source of truth for all project documents.

### 2.3. Terminal value

A single method — the Gordon model on a normalised cash flow (capital expenditure set equal to
depreciation, the increase in working capital equal to g × working capital).

- Normalised cash flow — **RUB 78.71 bn**; long-term growth rate g = 4% (nominal, equal to the inflation target).
- Terminal value at the end of 2036 = 78.71 × 1.04 / (0.2958 − 0.04) ≈ **RUB 320.0 bn**.
- Discounted to the valuation date (× 0.0749) — **RUB 23.98 bn**; share in enterprise value **38%**.

> Earlier editions used three incompatible approaches to terminal value at the same time (Gordon with
> g=2%, a three-stage model, and g=4% in the business plan). One has been retained.

### 2.5. Pre-money DCF (SoT v4)

## 11. Limitations and caveats

1. **IVS 2022 standard**: the valuation is performed using IVS 105–106 approaches.
2. **Reference date**: 24.05.2026. The pre-money is subject to review upon changes in the CBR key rate, the FX rate or the regulatory regime.
3. **Regulatory dependency**: the binary factor of adoption of the legislative package (horizon T+36 to T+60 months) may shift the Reasoned Value by a factor (from ×0.15 to ×1.5).
4. **Independence of the valuer**: the document is prepared as part of the project pack and is not an independent valuation report under 135-FZ. An independent appraiser registered with an SRO must be engaged for an official valuation.
5. **Wedge mode**: the project has a valid pilot-light configuration under the existing 259-FZ (without amendments to 86-FZ) — see `04_Бизнес-план/Wedge_pilot-light.md`.
